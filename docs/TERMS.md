# Terms of use

Skarb is open-source software intended to be self-hosted and used by a single individual
to manage their own personal finances.

- The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The owner runs it at their
  own risk and is responsible for securing the machine and database it runs on.
- Skarb accesses only bank accounts belonging to the person operating it, through official
  APIs and licensed open-banking providers, under consents that person grants directly at
  their bank.
- Skarb never initiates payments or moves money; access is read-only.
- Bank access tokens and keys are the owner's responsibility to keep confidential.
